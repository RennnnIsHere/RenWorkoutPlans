Progressive overload
The primary driver of muscle growth. Each week, aim to add 1 rep per set before increasing weight. This ensures continuous adaptation.
+1 rep → then +2.5 kg
Frequency & volume
Research (Schoenfeld, 2017) shows hitting each muscle 2× per week with 10–20 weekly sets is optimal for hypertrophy. Push/Pull/Legs + Upper achieves this.
10–20 sets/muscle/week
Rest periods
For hypertrophy, 90–180 seconds between sets is evidence-based. Longer rests (2–3 min) allow better performance on compound movements.
2 min compounds · 90s isolation
Recovery & sleep
Muscle protein synthesis peaks 24–48h post-workout. 7–9h sleep raises GH & testosterone, directly accelerating hypertrophy.
7–9 hrs sleep nightly
Protein intake
Meta-analyses (Morton, 2018) show ~1.6–2.2g/kg bodyweight/day maximizes muscle protein synthesis. Spread across 3–5 meals.
1.6–2.2 g/kg bodyweight/day
Range of motion
Training through full ROM produces greater hypertrophy than partial reps (Pedrosa, 2022). Focus on the stretch position.
Full stretch = max stimulus
