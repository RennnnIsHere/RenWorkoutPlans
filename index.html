Frequency & volume
Research (Schoenfeld, 2017) shows hitting each muscle 2× per week with 10–20 weekly sets is optimal for hypertrophy.

Rest periods
For hypertrophy, 90–180 seconds between sets is evidence-based. Longer rests allow better performance on compounds.
